Delete Your Account

⚠️ Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please read all information below before proceeding.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • All your data, including tables, projects, and settings will be permanently deleted
  • Your app access will be revoked
  • Any premium subscriptions will be cancelled
  • You will no longer receive support for this account
  • This action cannot be reversed or undone

⏱️ Deletion Timeline

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7-14 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

How to request account deletion

To comply with privacy regulations and ensure account security, we require account deletion requests to be made via email. This helps us verify your identity and process your request safely.

Click the button below to open your email client with a pre-filled deletion request. You can also manually send an email to our support team.

Support Email:

funtableshelp@gmail.com

If the button above doesn't work, you can manually copy this email address and send your deletion request.

Alternative: Contact Support

If you're experiencing issues with the app or have concerns, consider contacting our support team first. Many problems can be resolved without deleting your account.
